<SummarySection title="Summary">

Ganaderiapp is a mobile app, for iOS and Android, to run a cattle farm from the phone. It brings together the farm and its paddocks, with rotation, rest days and forage measurements, and the herd, with genealogy, weighings, photos, purchase and sale. It also covers health and reproduction, feeding, milk production, supply inventory and finances with receipts.

Reports can be exported as PDF, Excel or CSV for a bank, an accountant or a buyer. A farm can be shared with a team, with a role for each person, and the app sends alerts for vaccines, expected heat and births, low stock and paddocks that are ready. Animals, weighings and photos can be registered without a signal, and they sync when the phone is back online.

I designed and built it end to end. The app is in Spanish, with Colombian pesos and the Bogotá time zone.

</SummarySection>

<ChallengeSection title="Challenge & strategy">

### Challenge

A cattle farm in Colombia is usually run with a notebook, a spreadsheet and the memory of the person in charge. Which animal was vaccinated, when a cow is due, how much a paddock has rested, how much a supply costs and what the farm earned this month end up in different places, or in nobody's head. When the farm has partners, a foreman and a vet, the same information has to reach all of them, and each one should only change what they are responsible for.

The setting is also hard for software. Paddocks and corrals often have no signal, the phone is a mid-range Android used in the sun with one hand, and the people who need the data, such as a bank, an accountant or a buyer, do not use the app at all.

### Strategy

The idea was one app for the whole farm, built around how the work happens in the field. Everything that belongs to a farm is scoped to it: the paddocks, the herd, the supplies, the money and the team. The things that cannot wait for a connection, which are new animals, weighings and photos, can be registered offline and are sent later without duplicates.

Three rules shaped the design. The domain rules live in the database, so no screen can break them, and every operation that touches several tables is one transaction. Money follows the work: buying or selling an animal, a vaccine, an insemination, feeding or buying a supply creates its expense or income by itself. And the team model is simple: an owner, co-owners with permissions per module, and partners who can only read.

### Decisions

| Decision | Why | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Domain logic in PostgreSQL functions | Rules and transactions live next to the data, so no screen can skip them. | Business logic in SQL is harder to test and to debug than in application code. |
| Multi-tenancy by farm with Row Level Security | The database rejects any query outside the person's farms, even if the app has a bug. | Every table needs its policies, and a mistake in one is a data leak. |
| Partial offline mode | Only new animals, weighings and photos are queued, which is what the field needs most. | Health, feeding, milk, finances and paddocks need a connection, and there is no conflict merge. |
| Inventory by movements | The stock is a result of its history, never edited by hand, and never negative. | Corrections are new movements, not edits. |
| Automatic income and expenses | Nobody has to enter the same operation twice. | Deleting or editing the source does not always update the money entry. |
| Push queue in Postgres | Deliveries survive failures, retry and leave a trace, with no external queue. | At-least-once delivery and a dependency on `pg_cron` and `pg_net` being enabled by hand. |
| Reports built on the phone | No report server, and the file goes straight to the share sheet. | Large farms depend on the phone, and tables are capped at 20,000 rows. |
| Zustand and React Hook Form with Zod, no query cache library | Fewer moving parts, and the error handling lives in the services. | Caching and refetching are done by hand. |

<FeaturesSection title="Features">

Version 1.0 ships everything I set out to build, and it is organized the way a farm is.

| Area | What you can do |
|---|---|
| The farm and its pasture | Create farms and their paddocks, up to 50 at a time. Move the herd from paddock to paddock, track how many days each one has rested and record forage measurements. |
| The herd | Register animals with photos, buy and sell them, weigh them and see their daily gain. Follow their genealogy in a pedigree tree, and check the state of the herd from the home dashboard. |
| Care and production | Keep the health record and the vaccines, follow heat, inseminations and births, and log feeding and milk production. |
| Supplies and money | Keep the supply inventory, record income and expenses with a photo of the receipt, and see how the farm is doing in charts. |
| Everything around it | Sign in, invite the team, get alerts, work without a signal, and export reports and backups. |

Some details that make a difference day to day:

- **You do not type the money twice.** When you buy or sell an animal, apply a vaccine or a treatment, inseminate a cow, feed the herd or buy a supply, the expense or the income is recorded on its own. If the person doing it has no access to finances, the work is still saved and the money part is simply left out.
- **Each person sees what they should.** The owner can change everything, a co-owner only works in the modules the owner opened for them, and a partner can look but not touch. The server enforces it, and the app hides the buttons a person cannot use.
- **Alerts twice a day.** At 7 in the morning and 5 in the afternoon the app reminds you of vaccines due, expected heat and births, sick animals to check on, supplies running low and paddocks ready to graze, without repeating the same alert.
- **Your account is safe to leave.** You can pause your account and come back, or delete it with 15 days to change your mind and reminders 5 days and 1 day before it is gone.

<ResultsSection title="Results">

Ganaderiapp already does what I set out to build. You can create a farm, set up its paddocks and keep the herd in one place, with each animal's photos, weights, health, milk and family tree. Money gets tracked almost on its own, because buying an animal, giving a vaccine or buying feed creates its expense for you.

You can invite the people you work with and decide what each one can touch, get reminders for vaccines, births and low stock, and share a report as a PDF or an Excel file straight from your phone. And in the field, where there is often no signal, you can still register animals, weights and photos, and they sync when you are back online.

It is still in development, so I keep polishing it and testing it with demo data.

</ResultsSection>
